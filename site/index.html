Product

One window for everything you used to switch between.

Browsing, notes, terminals, diffs and agents share the same tabs, splits and spaces. Everything stays on your machine.

A real browser, built natively

Rust and GPU-rendered chrome over each platform's own engine: WebKit on macOS and Linux, WebView2 on Windows. Tabs, groups, spaces, nested splits, a dock and a command bar on ⌘K.

Pages

A block editor with slash commands, to-dos and code blocks. Agents can save their findings straight into a page.

Terminals and worktrees

Real PTYs, git worktree groups and live diffs beside the app you're building.

Documents, created and edited in-app

Agents produce PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files from one schema. Open them in a tab, edit the text, cells or slides and save. No office suite needed.

FAQ

Questions

Is it built on Chromium?

No. Hi-Fi uses the engine your OS already ships with, which is why it's about 10 MB and light on memory.

Do agents see my tabs?

No. Agents only see tabs they opened themselves. Your tabs, cookies and history stay out of reach unless you share a page.

Which models can I use?

Any model your harness supports, including free OpenCode models, Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok and local models.

Where is my data?

On your device. Pages, chats and sessions are plain files in your app data folder. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on sync, a planned Pro feature.
